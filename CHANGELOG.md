# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Added guided field and water source forms with read-only summary tables and field assignment on Water Supply.
- Added visible saved-field editing and deletion, linked help for technical terms, and a 100% desired relative yield default for new fields.
- Added field what-if curves and stacked irrigation charts, with a separate whole-farm scenario editor and comparison.
- Added editable CSU Extension ET starting estimates and separate grain, silage, and sweet corn presets.
- Simplified supply to expected farm delivery and added automatic import of v0.1.0 farm plans.

## 0.1.0

- Added the six-page local Streamlit planning workflow, editable field and source tables, and a hypothetical two-scenario demonstration farm.
- Implemented Maas–Hoffman crop response, FAO maintenance leaching, conditional effective leaching credit, conveyance accounting, and source-level and farm-level budgets.
- Added independent scenario editing and comparison, versioned JSON persistence, a multipage PDF management report, and CSV field results.
- Added verified crop and irrigation presets, scientific methods and reference registry, automated tests, optional logo configuration, and initial license documentation.
