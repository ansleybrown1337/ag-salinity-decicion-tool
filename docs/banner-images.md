# Banner image credits and prompts

The five banners in `static/banners/` were generated with OpenAI’s built-in image generation tool for Ag Salinity Manager. They depict illustrative agricultural scenes. The app serves cropped JPEGs locally.

## Shared prompt

Photorealistic agricultural editorial banner for a research tool. Natural muted greens, warm soil tones and restrained contrast, like a documentary photograph. Render a wide landscape frame. It will be center-cropped to a very shallow 8:1 strip, so keep all recognizable subjects distributed horizontally along the middle band. No text, numbers, logos, borders, overlays or watermarks. No people.

## Scene prompts

- [farm-setup.jpg](../static/banners/farm-setup.jpg) — A slightly elevated view across parallel rows of young irrigated crops and bronze soil on a working eastern Colorado farm, field edges and neighboring parcels entering the midground. Fine texture, realistic spacing, subtle dew and pale morning light.
- [farm-dashboard.jpg](../static/banners/farm-dashboard.jpg) — A low wide aerial photograph following a simple straight irrigation canal alongside green crop fields, with a narrow gravel bank and subtle reflections on dark blue-green water. Position the canal, field edges, and reflections near the center height so they read instantly as a slim banner.
- [field-decisions.jpg](../static/banners/field-decisions.jpg) — A low field-side photograph looking along orderly rows of healthy maize at leaf height, with sharp overlapping green leaves and a warm strip of soil and rows in the middle distance. Keep the characteristic maize leaves and row structure across the center height.
- [scenario-comparison.jpg](../static/banners/scenario-comparison.jpg) — A near-vertical drone photograph of adjacent High Plains farm parcels with straight borders. Neighboring fields show distinctive patterns of emerald crop rows, sage cover, and ochre-brown soil. The contrasting planted rectangles and agricultural texture remain visible in a narrow horizontal crop.
- [about-methods.jpg](../static/banners/about-methods.jpg) — A close editorial photograph of three neat, separate soil subsamples arranged horizontally in shallow stainless-steel trays on a clean muted sage workbench. Dry loam, fine tan soil, and slightly darker crumbly soil show authentic textures. Keep the samples in a horizontal row centered at the middle of the image. No labels or equipment displays.
